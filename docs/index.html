// features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

It uses the Spotify Web API, so it controls whichever device is active on your account: PC, phone or a smart speaker. The app's interface is in Italian.

🎧

Now playing

Album art, title, artist and album of what is playing on the active device.

⏯️

Playback controls

Play and pause, previous and next, shuffle, repeat (off, all, one).

🎚️

Seek bar and volume

Click or drag to jump anywhere in the song; volume slider and mute for the active device.

🖥️

Device switcher

Move playback between your PC, your phone or speakers such as an Amazon Echo.

⚙️

Your way

Screen corner, fade animation, transparency, album-art background, launch at startup. Hides by itself when you click elsewhere.

🔒

Secure login

OAuth with PKCE, no client secret. The refresh token is encrypted with Windows DPAPI. Automatic updates from GitHub Releases.

// setup

Connect your Spotify account

Spotify apps start in Development mode and only accept the users their owner added: that is why you need your own free app.

1

Create your Spotify app

In the Spotify Developer Dashboard, create an app, tick Web API and add the Redirect URI http://127.0.0.1:8888/callback.

2

Add yourself

Copy the Client ID (no secret needed) and, under User Management, add the email of your Spotify account.

3

Log in

Click the tray icon, paste the Client ID, then Accedi con Spotify (Log in with Spotify) and approve.

Installer or portable

The installer updates itself; the portable exe runs without installing and does not update.

powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\VERIFY.ps1
# or by hand, then compare with SHA256SUMS.txt:
Get-FileHash .\WidgetSpotify-Setup-x.y.z.exe -Algorithm SHA256

From the next release, SHA256SUMS.txt and VERIFY.ps1 come with every release. The exe is not code-signed: if SmartScreen warns you, choose More info → Run anyway.
